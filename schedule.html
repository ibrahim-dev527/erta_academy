FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to come every weekend? +

We recommend attending as regularly as possible for best progress, but we understand life gets busy. You can attend any available session and still make great progress with consistent effort.

Can beginners join at any time? +

Absolutely! Our beginner classes welcome new students at any time. Simply show up for a Saturday or Sunday session and our instructors will guide you from the very basics.

What should I bring to class? +

Wear comfortable sportswear or a Taekwondo dobok if you have one. Bring a water bottle and a towel. Beginners don't need any special equipment to start.

Are there classes for young kids? +

Yes! We have programs for children from age 4 onwards. Both Saturday and Sunday sessions include junior-specific classes taught by patient, experienced coaches.

How do I book a private lesson? +

Simply WhatsApp us at +233 248 533 250 to discuss availability, pricing, and scheduling for private one-on-one sessions with our certified instructors.

Is there a registration fee? +

For all fee and registration details, please reach out on WhatsApp (+233 248 533 250) or visit us in person at Kotobabi, Accra. We'll walk you through everything.
