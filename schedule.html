FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to come every weekend? +

We recommend attending as regularly as possible for best progress, but we understand life gets busy. You can attend any available session and still make great progress with consistent effort.

Can beginners join at any time? +

Absolutely! Our beginner classes welcome new students at any time. Simply show up for a Saturday or Sunday session and our instructors will guide you from the very basics.

What should I bring to class? +

Wear comfortable sportswear or a Taekwondo dobok (uniform) if you have one. Bring a water bottle, a towel, and an open mind. Beginners don't need any special equipment to start.

Are classes available for young kids? +

Yes! We have programs for children from age 4 onwards. Both Saturday and Sunday sessions include junior-specific classes taught by patient and experienced coaches.

How do I book a private lesson? +

Simply contact us on WhatsApp at +233 248 533 250 to discuss availability, pricing, and scheduling for private one-on-one sessions with our certified instructors.

Is there a registration fee? +

For all fee and registration details, please reach out to us on WhatsApp (+233 248 533 250) or visit us in person at Kotobabi, Accra. We'll be happy to walk you through everything.
