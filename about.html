Our Story

From the Ground Up
to Trophy Wins

Emperor Reigns Taekwondo Academy (ERTA) was founded with one clear vision: to create a space where every person — regardless of age or background — could develop physical strength, mental discipline, and genuine self-confidence through the art of Taekwondo.

What began as a small community training group in Kotobabi, Accra has grown into one of the city's most respected Taekwondo academies. Our students have competed and won medals at regional competitions, and our academy continues to produce champions — in sport and in life.

We believe Taekwondo is more than a martial art. It is a philosophy built on respect, perseverance, self-control, and indomitable spirit — values we instill in every student who trains with us.

Mission & Values

What We Stand For

🎯
Our Mission

To empower individuals of all ages through quality Taekwondo training, cultivating discipline, physical fitness, and mental resilience that extends beyond the mat into everyday life.

👁️
Our Vision

To be a leading Taekwondo academy in Accra, producing competitive athletes and well-rounded citizens who lead with honor and strength of character.

⭐
Our Promise

Every student receives dedicated coaching, a safe training environment, and the encouragement needed to unlock their fullest potential — from white belt onward.
